Straight answers

Is there an Android version?

No. iPhone only, iOS 15.1 or later. Whoever counts needs an iPhone.

Does it work in a dark bar?

Usually. For a very dark corner, step closer or use the phone's light.

What if it reads a bottle wrong?

Two AIs read every label. When they disagree, the app flags the bottle for a quick look instead of guessing. If one's wrong, remove that row and add the right bottle.

Does it connect to my POS?

No. It counts what's on the shelf and orders from that.

How do orders reach my distributors?

By email, to the rep or order address you add once.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. 15 days free, then $49.99/month. Cancel from the app.

Can I run more than one bar?

Yes. One account, each bar kept separate, no extra fee. Each bar keeps its own counts, prices and distributors.

Can my staff count?

Yes. Add their names and each count records who did it. No extra logins.
